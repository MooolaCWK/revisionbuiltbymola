02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
Elasticity measures proportional responsiveness

PED = percentage change in Qd ÷ percentage change in price

1
Example
Price rises 10%; quantity demanded falls 2%. PED ≈ -0.2.

2
Absolute value
|PED| means the magnitude, ignoring the minus sign: |PED| = 0.2.

3
Interpretation
A 1% price rise corresponds approximately to a 0.2% fall in quantity demanded.

Price

Other demand determinants must remain unchanged for this demand-curve interpretation.

(Mankiw, 2024)
