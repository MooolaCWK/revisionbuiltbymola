Same linear equation
Q = 500 - 2P. The slope stays constant.

Different proportions
For this line, point |PED| = 2P/Q.
At P = 200, Q = 100, |PED| = 4.

Key distinction
A 45-degree appearance does not establish unit elasticity.

Slope compares unit changes; elasticity compares percentage changes.
