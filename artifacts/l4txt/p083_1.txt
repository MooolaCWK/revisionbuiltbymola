Registration, headcount and FTE are different

Measure	What it tells us
Register entries	Registration or enrolment status; not necessarily current employment
Active headcount	Number of people actually working in the defined setting
Full-time equivalent (FTE)	Labour relative to a full-time workload
Example	0.5 → 0.5	1.0 FTE
Two nurses each working 0.5 of full-time hours = 1.0 FTE

Funded posts, vacancies, headcount and available nurse-hours answer different questions.
(Nursing Council of Hong Kong, 2026)
