Gross National Income (GNI) measures the total income earned by a country’s residents and businesses, no matter where it is produced.

Academic
GNI = GDP + primary income received from abroad − paid abroad

Gross National Income: Income earned by an economy’s residents, including net primary income from abroad.

Economic resident: An individual or organisation whose main economic base is in the economy.

Primary income: Income from work and ownership of assets, such as wages, interest and profits.

Residency is different from citizenship. Family remittances are not automatically primary income.
(Census and Statistics Department, 2026)
