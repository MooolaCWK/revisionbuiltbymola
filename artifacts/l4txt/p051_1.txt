01 / SUPPLY, DEMAND AND EQUILIBRIUM
A binding price ceiling creates excess demand

Ceiling: HK$60    Equilibrium price without the ceiling: HK$120

01
A price ceiling is a legal maximum price; this one is below equilibrium.

02
At P = 60:
Qd = 500 − 120 = 380;
Qs = −100 + 180 = 80.

03
Shortage = Qd − Qs = 380 − 80 = 300 consultations/month.

04
Waiting, eligibility rules or another allocation mechanism may result.

A ceiling at or above the equilibrium price is non-binding in this basic model.

(Mankiw, 2024)
