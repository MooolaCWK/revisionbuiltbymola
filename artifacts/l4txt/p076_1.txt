Aggregate demand is planned economy-wide spending

AD = C + I + G + (X − M)

• At each price level: AD describes the real output households, firms, government and overseas buyers plan to purchase.

• Healthcare examples: Private visits enter C; a private hospital building enters I; government purchases enter G.

• Accounting distinction: Expenditure GDP records actual expenditure. AD describes planned spending across price levels.

Government investment is included in G under the convention used in this lecture. (Mankiw, 2024)
