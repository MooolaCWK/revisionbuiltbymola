02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
A hypothetical nursing labour-supply calculation

Labour-supply elasticity = % change in L ÷ % change in w

01 L = nurses willing to work in the defined market; w = monthly wage.

02 Wage: HK$30,000 to HK$36,000.
Labour: 60,000 to 63,000 nurses.

03 Midpoint changes: 3,000 / 61,500 = 4.88%;
6,000 / 33,000 = 18.18%.

04 Elasticity = 4.88 / 18.18 ≈ 0.268.
Supply is inelastic in this example.

Approximately: a 1% wage increase corresponds to a 0.268% increase in labour supplied.

(Mankiw, 2024)
