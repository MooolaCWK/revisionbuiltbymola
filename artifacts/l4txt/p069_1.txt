Classifying domestic and cross-border income

Specified flow | Treatment for Hong Kong

HK-resident nurse earns wages working in Hong Kong | Domestic income generated by production; included in GNI

HK clinic earns profits attributable to nonresident owners | Production enters GDP; outward primary income lowers GNI relative to GDP

HK resident earns wages from a nonresident employer on a temporary overseas assignment | Inward primary income, assuming the worker remains an HK economic resident

Classify the income flow; do not exclude an entire firm because it is foreign-owned.

(Census and Statistics Department, 2026)
