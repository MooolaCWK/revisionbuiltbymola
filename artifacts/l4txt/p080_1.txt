Example: Lower confidence reduces planned consumption at each price level.

Hold SRAS unchanged: AD shifts left. Both equilibrium real GDP and the price level fall.

Government spending: Additional purchases can support AD; a budget announcement alone is not expenditure.

This is an economy-wide result under the stated short-run assumptions.
