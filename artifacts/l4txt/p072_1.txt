GDP measured through expenditure

GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)

C | Household final consumption
Private consultation

I | Private investment and inventories
Privately purchased new scanner

G | Government consumption and investment
Government purchase of care or hospital construction

X − M | Exports minus imports
Visitor care less imported devices

Count each purchase once. Cash transfers themselves are not purchases of output.
(Mankiw, 2024)
