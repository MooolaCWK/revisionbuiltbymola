Aggregate supply and potential output

SRAS
Short-run aggregate supply: real output firms are willing to produce at each price level in the short run.

LRAS and potential
Long-run aggregate supply marks sustainable potential output given labour, capital and technology.

Why upward sloping?
Some wages and other costs adjust slowly, so higher output prices can encourage production.

Potential output allows normal unemployment; it is not an absolute physical maximum.
(Mankiw, 2024)
