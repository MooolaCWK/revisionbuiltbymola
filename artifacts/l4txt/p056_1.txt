02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
Midpoint PED: two percentage changes

PED = [ (Q₂ - Q₁) / Q̄ ] ÷ [ (P₂ - P₁) / P̄ ]

Average quantity
Q̄ = (Q₁ + Q₂) / 2

Average price
P̄ = (P₁ + P₂) / 2

Consistent method
Use midpoint denominator for both changes.
The ×100 factors cancel.

Use midpoint elasticity for two-point calculations unless a question specifies otherwise.

(Mankiw, 2024)
