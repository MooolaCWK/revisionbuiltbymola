GDP measures production within an economy

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures the value of final goods and services produced within an economy during a stated period.

Final output: Output for final use. Avoid counting intermediate inputs again.

Value added: The value a producer adds after subtracting purchased intermediate inputs.

Healthcare example: Care produced by a foreign-owned clinic in Hong Kong contributes to Hong Kong GDP.

The owner’s citizenship does not determine where production is counted.

(Census and Statistics Department, 2026)
