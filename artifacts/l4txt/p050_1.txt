01 / SUPPLY, DEMAND AND EQUILIBRIUM
Solving equilibrium: a worked example

Qd = 500 – 2P and Qs = –100 + 3P

01 Set Qd = Qs: 500 – 2P = –100 + 3P

02 Collect terms: 600 = 5P, so P* = HK$120

03 Substitute: Q* = 500 – 2(120) = 260 visits/month

04 Check supply: –100 + 3(120) = 260 visits/month

Both schedules must give the same quantity at equilibrium.
