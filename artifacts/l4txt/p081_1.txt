The unemployment rate uses the labour force

Unemployment rate = U / (E + U) × 100

E: employed:
People in
employment.

U: unemployed:
Normally without
work, available and
seeking work;
official definitions
include exceptions.

Labour force = employed + unemployed.
It is not the whole population.

Worked example:
E = 3.8 million;
U = 0.2 million.
