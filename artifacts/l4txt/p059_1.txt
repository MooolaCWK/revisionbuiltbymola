02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
Elasticity and total revenue

Before: 400 × 80 = HK$32,000    After: 500 × 52 = HK$26,000

Demand over the relevant change | If price rises | If price falls
Inelastic | Revenue rises | Revenue falls
Unit elastic | Revenue unchanged | Revenue unchanged
Elastic | Revenue falls | Revenue rises

For discrete changes, use midpoint elasticity over that interval. Costs are needed for profit.
(Mankiw, 2024)
