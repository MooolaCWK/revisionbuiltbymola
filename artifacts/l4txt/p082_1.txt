Participation and underemployment

80%

Labour-force participation
Labour-force participation
Labour force ÷ corresponding population aged 15+ × 100.
Example: 4 million ÷ 5 million × 100 = 80%.

Time-related underemployment
The person has work but insufficient hours under the official criteria.
Voluntary part-time work alone does not establish underemployment.

An unavailable non-working person is generally outside the labour force.
(Census and Statistics Department, 2025)
