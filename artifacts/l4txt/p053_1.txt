02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
Elastic, unit elastic and inelastic demand

Magnitude | Meaning | For a small price rise

|PED| < 1
Inelastic: Q changes
proportionately less
Revenue rises

|PED| = 1
Unit elastic: equal
proportional response
Revenue is locally
unchanged

|PED| > 1
Elastic: Q changes
proportionately more
Revenue falls

Revenue = price × quantity. Revenue is different from profit.
