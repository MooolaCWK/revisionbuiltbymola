02 / PRICE ELASTICITY
Supply elasticity depends on the time horizon

Supply elasticity = % change in quantity supplied ÷ % change in price

Short run
Available hours may change through overtime, retention or return to practice. Training and facilities limit rapid expansion.

Longer run
Training, migration, facilities and technology can expand service capacity.

An inelastic supply curve has a small quantity response to a given percentage price change.

(Folland et al., 2024)
