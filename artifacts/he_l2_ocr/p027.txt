What p = 0.001 does not mean
THE BIG MISCONCEPTION
Students often hearp = 0.001 as:
"there is onlya 0.1% chance the
coin isfair."
That iswrong.

To compute thep-valueyou already
pretended the coin was 100%fair.
Thep-value cannotbe theprobability the
coin is fair-that assumption was the
starting line.It only says how poorly the
real tosses match that pretend scenario.
