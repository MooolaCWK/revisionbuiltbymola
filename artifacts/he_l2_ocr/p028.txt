Cheat sheet for students
Low p(e.g.p≤0.05)
If thedefault assumptionwere
true,these datawould be
highly unusual.
Action:treat the default asa
poor fit for these data.
Convention often says"reject."
That isnot proof the
alternative is true,and nota
5%chance the default is true.

High p (e.g. p > 0.05)
Thesedata arereasonably
compatiblewith thedefault.
Thispattern could easilyhappen
by chance under that story.
Action:do not reject the
default.That isnot the same as
proving the coin isfair,or
“keeping”the assumption as
true.
（Flechner&Tseng,2011;Greenland et al.,2016)
