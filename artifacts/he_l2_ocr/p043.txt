Choosing a Regression Specification
Match the model to the outcome type.Identification is a separate choice.
Model Outcome
Linear (OLS) Continuous
Logistic Binary→probability
Poisson/negativebinomial Counts
Cox Time-to-event
Designmodel.To estimate the effect of a policy threshold you need a design (RCT,RD,difference-in-
differences).RD is not another kind of Y-and it usually still uses a linear or polynomial regression.
Cost caveat:hospital charges are skewed.Raw-scale OLS on charges is often a poor fit.In practice,
cost models often use a GLM(e.g.gamma with log link) rather than plain linear regression.
（Bzovskyet al.,2022;Zhouetal.,2023;Tanniet al,2020;Skrepnek,2005)
